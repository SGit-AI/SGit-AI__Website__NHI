Entro Security

Secrets-centric NHI security: where every secret lives, who owns it, what it reaches. Group: NHI discovery, posture & governance.

Profile status

Compiled from published analyses — principally the Aembit vendor guide and the 2026 NHI tools survey — and the vendor's own materials. Date verified: 18 August 2026. Not a hands-on assessment; capabilities and pricing move monthly and corrections are welcome via comms.

What it does

Entro takes a secrets-first approach to NHI security: mapping agents to credentials, tracing where secrets live and who owns them, detecting exposure, managing posture, and monitoring behaviour for detection and response.

Key capabilities

  • Agent-to-credential mapping
  • Ownership tracking
  • Secrets exposure detection
  • Posture management
  • Behaviour monitoring, detection and response

Which part of the question it answers

Discovery-and-posture layer, entered through the secrets. The cited analysis notes it may need pairing with separate workload authentication and preventive access enforcement.

And for rented agents?

Directly relevant to the do-nothing baseline: it finds and tracks the broad credentials rented agents already hold. Visibility over the hope rather than a scoped identity.
