SailPoint

Identity governance extended to agents: ownership, certification, lifecycle. Group: NHI discovery, posture & governance.

Profile status

Compiled from published analyses — principally the Aembit vendor guide and the 2026 NHI tools survey — and the vendor's own materials. Date verified: 18 August 2026. Not a hands-on assessment; capabilities and pricing move monthly and corrections are welcome via comms.

What it does

SailPoint's Agent Identity Security applies its identity-governance machinery to agents: discovery, accountable ownership, access-pathway mapping, entitlement certification, and lifecycle governance alongside human and non-human identities.

Key capabilities

  • Agent discovery and inventory
  • Accountable ownership
  • Access-pathway mapping
  • Entitlement reviews and certification
  • Lifecycle governance

Which part of the question it answers

Governance layer. The cited analysis notes governance establishes approved access; a separate control must apply those decisions when the agent actually connects.

And for rented agents?

Ownership and lifecycle governance are precisely what the CSA survey found missing for agent identities; applies to rented agents' credentials as records, not to the agents as workloads.
