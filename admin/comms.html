  • Decisions log: decisions taken so far — MVP ships static HTML without the vault bridge (T12 queues the vault pattern); options assessed as preliminary-from-sources with explicit evidence-status boxes rather than waiting for hands-on re-runs; infographics linked not mirrored until N2 is decided; source briefs are captured verbatim under briefs/ with a presentation page under research/, so the raw document and the curated view coexist — which is itself the provenance discipline the shared-drives brief argues for.
